 Experience It

Xinyi Shen

West Coast Smoker by Fall Out Boy

[1:52 - 2:22]

I chose this song because of its repeatability I have a habit of playing my current favorite on loop until I get sick of it. This one hasn’t worn out on me yet.

I first heard about this album from a friend back in middle school, but at the time, I didn’t fully understand the song since I had just moved to America. What stuck with me, though, was the album cover—it’s pretty distinctive. Then, one day, YouTube randomly recommended one of the songs to me, and it all came back.
