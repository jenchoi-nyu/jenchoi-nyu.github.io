Fading Memories
by Linnea Olsson

[1:05–1:35]

View Parametric Models ↗

I generally enjoy indie games, especially how they integrate music into the experience. Sayonara Wild Hearts stood out to me because of its incredible soundtrack. My brother introduced me to the game and I instantly loved the hyperpop tracks.

Later on, I discovered the demo version of my favorite OST from the game. It had a much calmer, more acoustic feel, and I found myself listening to it while working or taking a break because it was so relaxing.

Since the demo track was a limited edition release on a physical cassette tape, the digital version is only available on YouTube.
