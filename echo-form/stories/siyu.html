Siyu Liu

Ballade No.1 in G minor, Op.23
by Frédéric Chopin

[4:40–5:18]

View Parametric Models ↗

This piece has been with me for most of my life—I started learning piano when I was just four years old. It was the first piece of music that could make me raise my eyebrows in awe and also make me want to cry.

I listen to it all the time—whether I’m working on assignments, sleeping, commuting, or even working out. The peaceful parts help me focus and relax, but my favorite section (4:40–5:18) always gives me an energy boost!

I love a wide range of music genres, but this is one piece I’ve never gotten tired of, no matter how many years have passed.
