Take Me
by G-Dragon

[1:16–1:52]

View Parametric Models ↗

I've been a huge fan of GD since I was a kid and my mom loves Big Bang's songs too. Most of my friends are GD fans as well, so we always end up singing his songs at karaoke.

Lately, Take Me has been my favorite—it’s got beautiful lyrics and a rhythm that makes me think about the feeling of being loved by someone special.

After seven years of waiting, G-Dragon finally released a new album, making this song even more meaningful for me and so many others.
